RHODES WEDDING PHOTOGRAPHY

Wedding photography for couples from Greece and abroad

Pardalos Photos & Videos photographs weddings and destination weddings across Rhodes. From preparations and the ceremony to portraits, family photographs and the reception, our goal is to preserve the atmosphere and genuine emotion of your day.

We are based in Afantou and cover wedding locations across the island, including Lindos, Faliraki, Ialysos and Afantou. Photography can be combined with professional wedding videography and drone coverage.
